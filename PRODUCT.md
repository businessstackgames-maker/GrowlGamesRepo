# PRODUCT.md — SatoshiSpin

## What it is
SatoshiSpin is a **crypto casino lobby** — a free-to-play + crypto-deposit gaming
platform. This surface is the signed-out **lobby**: the storefront where players
browse game categories, see live promotions, and start playing or sign up.

## Register
**Product** (the lobby is task UI: sidebar nav + game grids), carrying **brand**
energy in the hero promos and CTAs. The tool should feel fast and trustworthy
first, exciting second.

## Users
Crypto-savvy casual players. Multi-currency (BTC, ETH, USDT, DOGE, TRX, …).
Heavily mobile, but the desktop lobby is the showcase. They scan fast, chase
bonuses, and care about: which games are hot, what they can win, how fast they
can deposit.

## Primary jobs-to-be-done
1. Browse and find a game fast (categories, search, "hottest/new").
2. Understand the current promotion (Weekly Wager Rush) and prize pool.
3. Sign up / log in / deposit with minimal friction.
4. Track balances across currencies (wallet).

## Brand
- **Mood:** neon arcade on carbon — energetic, premium, a little electric.
- **Base:** `#1C1C1C` (everything sits on the darkest brand color).
- **Accents:** brand neons used as accents only, never as the body surface.
- **Type:** **Poppins**, across the entire product (brand requirement).
- **Logo:** text-only "SatoshiSpin" wordmark, white, Poppins (mixed-case, 800).

## Voice
Bold, concise, confident. No exclamation-mark hype, no "Oops!", no AI clichés
("Elevate", "Seamless", "Unleash"). Plain, specific, a little swagger.

## Out of scope (this pass)
Real game launches, real payments/auth backend, account pages. CTAs open
realistic front-end modals/toasts; no server.
